Intro

9/16/26 · Personal Note · 1 min · #notes

Overfit is my public research notebook for prediction markets, trading bots, and probability.

I am a freshman at Ohio University studying mathematics with a minor in computer science. I am building market experiments in public because most ideas sound cleaner before they meet data. The point of this site is to document the setup, the assumptions, the result, and the lesson instead of only writing about ideas after they look good.

I will write about prediction markets, crypto market structure, paper trading bots, calibration, expected value, break-even math, and postmortems from experiments that did not work. When possible, I will include sample sizes, assumptions, PnL, and the parts of the process that were weaker than expected.

This is not financial advice, a signal service, or a trading brand. It is a record of what I am testing and what I am learning from the results.
